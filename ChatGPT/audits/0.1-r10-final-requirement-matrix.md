## Release-impact conclusion

The three `CONTRADICTED` rows and five related `PARTIAL` rows all trace to one
release blocker: authenticated principal resolution and current-value service
composition share one SQLAlchemy session. The principal lookup begins a read
transaction; the D1 selector correctly refuses to start from a non-idle session.
The result is a safe but unconditional 409 at both active current endpoints.

Required remediation: **0.1-R10-E1 — current-value authenticated
session/transaction composition**.
