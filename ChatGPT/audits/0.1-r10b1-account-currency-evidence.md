## Blocker mapping

R10-B showed that original-currency breakdowns are not account-currency totals
for a mixed-native account and that liability-native evidence was not

## Physical design and authority

For `Account.currency != User.baseCurrency`, one writer transaction creates or
replays a primary snapshot in the user base currency and an account-currency
companion. The pair shares account, timestamp, granularity, source,
calculation version, and canonical evidence. Each row has its own deterministic
ID and complete `AccountSnapshotItem` set.

The primary remains the sole input to NetWorth, portfolio aggregate, dashboard
global finance, and history. The companion is persisted evidence for the
future R10-B2 selected-account and dashboard-account presentation. A
same-currency account has one row only.

## Exact CZK-pivot contract

The ČNB provider still owns only direct `foreign currency -> CZK`
observations. Snapshot calculation composes persisted legs at write time:

- identity conversion consumes no rate;
- foreign-to-CZK consumes its direct rate;
- CZK-to-foreign consumes the target foreign-to-CZK rate;
- foreign-to-foreign consumes source-to-CZK and target-to-CZK.

Foreign-to-foreign calculation evaluates
`amount * source_to_czk / target_to_czk` once in a high-precision Decimal
context. The final MONEY value must be exactly representable. There is no
rounding repair and no synthetic cross-rate row. Every leg used by a pivoted
account-currency valuation must be a persisted ČNB observation; another
provider fails closed.

Internal `exchangeRates` audit version 2 records exact direct observation IDs
with `pivot_source` and `pivot_target` roles. Historical metrics use
event-date source and target legs under the same canonical as-of policy.
Direct-only primary audit payloads preserve their existing version 1 contract.

## Required scenarios

- Mixed EUR/USD investment: a CZK primary and EUR companion persist exact
  value and cost basis; both direct ČNB IDs are in companion lineage; no
  `USD -> EUR` observation exists.
- Liability: the CZK primary stores the converted liability and the EUR
  companion stores the exact native EUR liability in physical
  `liabilitiesValue`.
- Same currency: one physical snapshot only and no self-FX evidence.
- Mixed cash: identity and pivoted components contribute to the exact
  account-currency companion while native breakdowns remain unchanged.
- Historical metrics: net deposits, realized P/L, fees, and taxes consume
  event-date pivot legs rather than snapshot-time rates.
- Items: companion item `value` and `costBasis` are in `Account.currency`;
  native fields preserve their original denomination.

## Atomicity, replay, and concurrency

Currency identities are locked in canonical sorted order. Canonical evidence
is locked once, both projections and immutable existing rows are validated
before insert, and both rows and their items share one transaction. Missing,
stale, future, wrong-provider, wrong-pair, conflicting, or
non-representable evidence produces neither new primary nor companion.

Exact replay leaves physical row counts unchanged. Concurrent identical
refreshes converge to one primary and one companion without a deadlock or
partial pair.

## Primary parity and isolation
