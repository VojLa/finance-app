## Required remediation

`0.1-R5-B4 — exact provider alias onboarding`

The follow-up must provide an explicit, server-owned and tested production
mechanism to create or reconcile exact CoinGecko and Twelve Data identities.
It must not infer identities from ticker, ISIN, name, broker symbol, Listing
MIC, exchange, a first-alias fallback, or a hard-coded `BTC -> bitcoin` map.
