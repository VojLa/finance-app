the TS cash/FX calculation, and the TS Raiffeisenbank parser/database preview from the
registered Next.js route tree. Current account list/create/update/archive remain thin,
generated-contract Python adapters.

remain registered and authoritative. No replacement Next route was added because no
current product flow consumes that capability. A future sharing UI must use a thin
adapter over those Python endpoints rather than restoring the deleted direct-write
routes.

`src/lib/accountAccess.ts` cannot yet be deleted: its observed consumers are the
still-active legacy transaction, operational dashboard, manual investment, portfolio,
snapshot, rates, and import utility paths assigned to R11-E through R11-I. Removing it
now would break active flows. Each owning cutover must remove its consumer; R11-L will
enforce zero remaining production imports.
