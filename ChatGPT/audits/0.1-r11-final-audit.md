## Remaining risks

- Twelve Data licensing and quota suitability must be re-checked before a
  commercial or public deployment. Technical access is not legal approval.
- External-provider availability remains fail-closed; missing or stale evidence
  intentionally produces an unavailable state rather than a synthetic value.
- The five frontend skips and 667 backend skips are environment-gated suites;
  the release-critical PostgreSQL subset was run separately with zero skips.
- `next lint` is deprecated by Next.js and should be migrated to the ESLint CLI
  during a later tooling-only roadmap item.
