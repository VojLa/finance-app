## Old and new active workflows

Before D2, both current pages called snapshot refresh, persisted a complete
current-clock minute AccountSnapshot/NetWorthSnapshot graph, and then read its
exact manifest. D2 replaces that active browser path with:

```text
authenticated current intent
→ server-owned minute-aligned asOf
→ newest complete D1 daily baseline
→ exact canonical forward roots
→ trusted market requirement planning
→ provider I/O and atomic market evidence persistence
→ REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY revalidation
→ ephemeral primary and presentation projections
→ portfolio/dashboard response
```

The new internal Python endpoints are `POST /api/v1/portfolio/current` and
`POST /api/v1/dashboard/current`. They accept no financial lineage. The thin
Next adapters mint a fresh internal token and make one current request; they do
not invoke snapshot refresh, Prisma finance, rates, or legacy finance.

## Baseline, delta, and failure ownership

`DailySnapshotBaselineService` remains the sole selector. Planning and final
projection both revalidate the same exact baseline, account configuration,
canonical revisions, primary/presentation identities, and post-baseline change
set. A root affects current state only when its revision is above the D1 cutoff
and its financial timestamp is after the baseline and at or before `asOf`.

The newest invalid candidate fails closed. Missing baselines, backfills,
configuration/version drift, missing companion lineage, corrupt roots, and
missing/conflicting market evidence return the safe 409 unavailable contract.
There is no older, nearest, full-history, current-Holding, zero, or implicit
minute-snapshot fallback.

## Financial projection

- Cash: persisted baseline native balances plus signed forward Transactions.
- Investments: persisted baseline items seed the shared canonical Holding
  movement rules; only forward InvestmentEvents and exact movement sets advance
  quantity, native average cost, cost basis, investment cash, and closed/new
  positions. Production D2 never consumes current Holding finance.
- Historical metrics: baseline net deposits, realized P/L, fees, and taxes plus
  forward event-date FX evidence. Baseline history is never reconverted.
- Unrealized P/L: reconstructed current quantity/native cost valued by exact
  current price and FX evidence.
- Liabilities: exact baseline point or latest unique eligible forward
  LiabilityBalance replacement, never an additive delta.

Market requirements are derived from reconstructed open listings and persisted
listing/asset/alias identity. Provider transport runs outside a finance read
transaction. Final projection consumes persisted PriceSnapshot and direct CNB
ExchangeRate evidence; foreign-to-foreign output uses the established exact
CZK pivot without synthetic cross-rate rows or intermediate rounding.

One canonical state is projected into User.baseCurrency primary finance and
Account.currency presentation finance. Aggregate portfolio, dashboard,
allocation, positions, and ephemeral NetWorth use primaries only. Account views
and cards use the presentation projection. Dashboard is derived from the same
portfolio result and is not a second financial engine.

## Persistence and history

Normal current reads do not insert or update AccountSnapshot,
AccountSnapshotItem, AccountSnapshotCanonicalBoundary, NetWorthSnapshot,
DailySnapshotBaseline, DailySnapshotBaselineAccount, canonical finance, or
Holding rows. Only approved PriceSnapshot/ExchangeRate acquisition may persist.
Explicit/scheduled daily snapshot creation remains unchanged and continues to
produce the D1 baseline machinery.

History remains the persisted NetWorthSnapshot series. The current response
returns the selected baseline NetWorth identity solely as a history anchor; no
synthetic current history point is created. Current displayed finance may be
newer than the latest persisted chart point.
