  header. Redirects, retries, fallback, discovery, inverse, pivot, and partial
  repair are absent.

  and financial metrics now consume direct output-currency evidence.
- The CNB adapter and tests, Yahoo environment flag, and `yahoo-finance2`
  dependency were removed.
- The read-only audit now also counts snapshots that reference legacy rate IDs;
  reconciliation and restore instructions preserve immutable history.
